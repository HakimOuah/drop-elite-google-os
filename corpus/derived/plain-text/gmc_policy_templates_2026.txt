will assist you throughout the process. Restocking Fee: $0 - we do not charge any restocking fees for
returns.
Return Policy Overview
Return Window: You have 30 days from the day you receive your order to return items.
Condition: Items must be unused, in their original condition, and returned with the original packaging,
tags, and accessories.
Easy Returns: Our support team will assist you throughout the process.
Restocking Fee: $0 - we do not charge any restocking fees for returns.
Return Costs
For Defective or Incorrect Products: No cost to the customer. This applies when the product is
incorrect, damaged by the carrier, or defective in any other way.
For Customer Remorse (Change of Mind): The customer pays the actual return shipping cost. This
applies when the customer orders the wrong item, it doesn't fit, or they no longer want it. Shipping costs
are non-refundable.
How to Return an Item
1. Contact Us: Email [Support Email] with your order number and reason for the return.
2. Pack Your Item: Repack the item securely in its original packaging, including all accessories, tags,
and documents.
3. Send It Back: Ship your return using the instructions provided in your return authorization email. You
may use the carrier or shipping method recommended in that email.
Refund Process
Inspection: Once we receive your return, we'll inspect the item to ensure it meets our return criteria.
Refund Timing: After approval, your refund will be processed. It typically takes [Up to 7 business days]
for the refund to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank or payment
provider.
