PHASE 3
Scaling (Profit-Based)
Scaling decisions are made using profit and trustworthy data.
Green Days vs Red Days
GREEN DAY
Net profitable after ad spend for that day
RED DAY
Net unprofitable after ad spend for that day
Revenue - ad spend = the decision metric.
ROAS supports context, but profit is the final decision-maker. Use reliable tracking tools like TrackBee
to verify data accuracy.
SCALE UP - 2 GREEN DAYS RULE
Scale UP when:
• You have 2 green days in a row
• PMAX is spending normally
• No tracking or site issues exist
Recommended increase: 20-30% per step

Examples: $120 → $160 (~33%) | $160 → $220 (~37%) | $220 → $300+ (~36%)

SCALE BACK - 2 RED DAYS RULE
Scale DOWN when:
• You have 2 red days in a row
• Net profit turns negative after ad spend
• No clear external issue exists
Recommended decrease: 20-30%, or return to last profitable level
This prevents emotional reactions to single bad days while protecting capital.

Product Selection Guidelines
Products that scale best:
• Low return risk
• No sizing complexity
• Simple buying decision
Examples: Sunglasses | Watches | Men's fashion basics
Lower returns = safer scaling.
Common Mistakes to Avoid
• Multiple PMAX campaigns too early
• Scaling before AOV is fixed
• Trusting Shopify analytics blindly
• Resetting campaigns frequently
• Scaling without profit discipline
