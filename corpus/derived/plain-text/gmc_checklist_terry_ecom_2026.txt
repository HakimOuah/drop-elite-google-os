This framework is not for:

Do not skim this document.
You should:
• Review every section carefully
• Verify your store against each checklist item
• Fix issues before submitting or resubmitting products

• Instant disapprovals
• Silent suspensions
• Repeated policy flags

Understanding Google's Risk Windows
Google Merchant Center accounts are most sensitive during the following periods:
I First 48 Hours
• Automated systems scan your domain, products, policies, and business signals
• Many accounts fail here due to incomplete setup
I First 7 Days
• Deeper automated checks and potential human review
• Policy mismatches are often caught at this stage
I First 30 Days
• Ongoing monitoring
• Sudden or unnecessary changes can trigger reviews or suspensions
I Risk Warning
Approval does not mean safety. Most suspensions happen after approval, not before.

Store Isolation Framework
(Critical for Multiple Stores)
If you operate more than one store, isolation is essential.
Recommended Structure (Per Store)
Each store should have:
• One Google Merchant Center account
• One Google Ads account
• One dedicated Gmail address
• One unique payment method
• One virtual or physical address
• One local phone number
• One clean IP or proxy
• One isolated browser profile (recommended)
I Risk Warning
Do not reuse details across stores. Even small overlaps can create account linkage.
Multi-store linkage is one of the most common causes of repeated GMC suspensions.

Branding, Trust & Transparency
Google evaluates legitimacy, not aesthetics.
Compliance Checklist
• Use ™ or © symbols only if you legally own the rights
• Avoid fake urgency (false scarcity, misleading countdowns)
• Make contact details clickable (email and phone)
• Display contact information clearly in footer, contact page, and policy pages
• Use a professional business email address
Your store must look and function like a real business, not a temporary storefront.

Product Pages & Images
Product presentation is one of the most common rejection areas.
Image Rules
• No text overlays on product images
• No collage-style images
• No duplicate images across products
• No misleading visuals
Product Content
• Product title must match the product URL
• Titles and descriptions must be original
• Avoid exaggerated or unverifiable claims
• No fake or manipulated reviews
Many misrepresentation flags originate from title, image, or description mismatches.

Website Technical Alignment
Small technical mismatches cause big problems.
Technical Checklist
• Page speed score above 65
• Remove old or duplicate GMC verification codes
• Avoid duplicated themes across multiple stores
• Shopify settings must match footer, policy content, and business details
• Payment icons must match checkout options
• No broken links
• Archive out-of-stock products
I Risk Warning
Inconsistencies across pages are a common review trigger.
Automated scans can detect mismatches that are easy to miss during manual review.

Shopify & Policy Consistency

• Merchant Center business details
All must align.
Even small differences (address formatting, payment methods, refund terms) can result in:
• Disapproval
• Account review
• Suspension

Final Pre-Submission Checklist
Before submitting or resubmitting products, confirm:
• All contact details are visible and clickable
• Policies are complete and consistent
• Product titles and URLs match
• Images meet Google standards
• No misleading claims or urgency
• Technical setup is clean
• No reused data across stores
 Avoid
If any item fails → do not submit yet.
Some sellers choose to run an additional automated compliance scan before submission to identify
silent risk signals.

Ongoing Monitoring (After Approval)
Approval is not the end.
During the first 30 days:
• Avoid sudden changes
• Monitor product disapprovals closely
• Keep business details stable
• Recheck this checklist if issues arise
Ongoing monitoring tools can help surface issues before they escalate into suspensions.
