How to Use This Framework
Read and apply sections in order. Do not skip ahead. Most failures happen before GMC creation or
during rushed reviews. Each section is written as pass / fail guidance.
SECTION 1 - How Google Actually Reviews Stores
Google does not approve stores based on effort, explanations, or intent. Approval is driven by an
internal trust-scoring system that evaluates consistency, legitimacy, and risk signals across your
store, Google Merchant Center, and supporting assets.
What actually happens during review:
- Google crawls your site and GMC as a machine first, human second
- Text, structure, metadata, and linkage are compared automatically
- Human reviewers verify only what automation already flagged
Critical realities observed across 500+ reviews:
- Misrepresentation is cumulative, not a single mistake
- Small inconsistencies compound into failure
- Some non-compliant stores remain approved temporarily, then auto-reviewed
- Explanations do not override mismatches
Core mindset: look, act, and operate like a legitimate brand that ships what it sells - because Google
is scoring signals, not stories.

SECTION 2 - Fast-Track Approval Principles (Non-
Negotiable)
These principles are enforced regardless of niche, country, or store size. If any are violated, approval
odds drop sharply.
- One store = one identity (never reuse Gmail, phone, address, IP, or content)
- Consistency beats perfection (matching matters more than wording quality)
- Fewer GMC reviews = higher success rate (do not rush or repeat review requests blindly)
- Google compares text, not intent (explanations do not help)
- Google scores “trust assets” across your store. If key pages are empty (thin About/Policies, 1–2 
product collections), hidden (policies not linked in footer / hard to access on mobile / noindex), or 
duplicated (same policies/about/contact across other domains), Google reads it as a 
nonlegitimate setup and downgrades trust.
If any principle fails, stop and fix before proceeding.

SECTION 3 - Pre-Build Setup (Before Shopify or GMC
Exists)
Most GMC failures originate here. Complete all items before building a store or creating a Merchant
Center.
Domain Preparation
- New domains can be approved
- Domains aged 30+ days materially improve trust
- Deleted domains reset age to zero - buy only active, non-deleted domains
Gmail Preparation (Often Missed)
- Create a fresh Gmail used only for GMC and Google Ads
- Warm the Gmail for several days:
- Subscribe to services
- Watch YouTube
- Log into Google apps
- Send and receive emails
Google evaluates Gmail behaviour, not just existence.
Phone Number Strategy
- Physical SIM cards outperform VoIP numbers
- Same number must be used for:
- Gmail verification
- GMC contact details
- Store footer
- Country mismatch is allowed if the number accepts voice calls
Environment Isolation


--- PAGE ---

- Use proxy + anti-detect only for Gmail, GMC, and Ads
- Never access Shopify inside a proxy (high ban risk)
If this section is incomplete, do not proceed.

SECTION 4 - Store Build Requirements (Trust Signals)
This section determines whether Google perceives your store as legitimate or risky.
Contact & Identity (Footer-First Rule)
- Clickable email using mailto:
- Voice-capable phone number
- Real, mappable address
- Exact consistency across store, policies, and GMC
Reviewers often check the footer only.
Address Strategy (Critical for Multi-Store Operators)
- Never reuse addresses across stores
- Store address must match GMC
- Verification documents do not need to match store address
Addresses function as isolation tools, not proof of identity.
About Us Page
- Short, simple, human-written copy
- Avoid AI-detectable text
- No exaggerated history, claims, or credentials
Social Media (High-Risk When Done Early)
- No social links > weak or new social pages
- Purchased followers or low activity reduce trust
- Link socials only after real activity exists
External Trust Signals


--- PAGE ---

- If Trustpilot exists, rating must be 3.0+ before any review
- Trustpilot below 3.0 is a hard rejection gate

Google treats empty or thin collections as a quality signal.
Collections
- Minimum 5 products per collection
- No empty or hidden collections
Products
- Unique titles, descriptions, SKUs
- No text or collages on images
- Variant images must match selection
- Region-correct specs and sizing
Technical
- Redirect all 404s
- Avoid embedded images in descriptions

SECTION 7 - GMC Creation & Verification Blueprint
Fast-Track Creation Flow (Correct Order)
1. Store Finished
2. Policies Finalised (Shopify)
3. Products Uploaded
4. Create GMC
5. Verify & Claim Domain
6. Match Store Policies Word-for-Word
7. Connect Product Feed
8. Request Review
Never create GMC before the store is complete. Google can index incomplete pages.
Domain Verification
- Use DNS TXT record method (most reliable)
- HTML tag and file upload are secondary options
- Verify HTTPS version of domain
Policy Matching (Critical)
- Copy exact wording from Shopify -> Settings -> Policies
- Do not reword or simplify
- Paste directly into GMC policy fields
Feed Connection
- Use Simprosys app on Shopify
- Ensure all products are approved in feed


--- PAGE ---

- Fix disapprovals before requesting review

SECTION 8 - Review Submission & Post-Approval
Before Requesting Review
- Run complete self-audit using GMC Scout or manual checklist
- Verify footer contact details match GMC exactly
- Check all policy pages are accessible
- Ensure no 404 errors exist
- Test phone number accepts voice calls
Review Timeline
- Initial review: 3-5 business days
- Re-review after rejection: 7-10 days minimum
- Multiple rapid reviews reduce success rate
If Rejected
- Read rejection reason carefully
- Fix ALL issues, not just the stated one
- Wait 7-10 days before re-submitting
- Do not appeal without fixing underlying issues
Post-Approval Maintenance
- Google can re-review at any time
- Maintain policy consistency
- Keep contact details updated
- Monitor for policy violations in feed
